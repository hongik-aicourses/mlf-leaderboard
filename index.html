The score

The total negative log2-likelihood of the stories divided by their size in bytes. The stories are TinyStories-style stories, stories in many genres and fables, with more varied words than TinyStories.

Live board and final ranking

This board shows each team's best score. The final ranking scores each team's last accepted upload before the deadline once, on a separate private set.

Rules

At most 25 M parameters and 100 MB, causal models only, weights trained from scratch (no pretrained models and no logit distillation; pretrained tokenizers are fine), any training data including text written by other models, two scored uploads per team per day (three from Sat 28 Nov to the freeze). The baselines are trained on TinyStories only.

Bonus

Score bonus from your final score: below 1.46 +0.5%, below 1.20 +1.0%, below 1.00 +1.5% (highest tier only). Rank bonus on top: 1st +1.0%, 2nd and 3rd +0.5%; final scores within 0.01 bpb count as tied. The final score is your last upload before the deadline, scored once on the private set, so it can differ slightly from this board.

Uploads are scored within a few minutes of arriving. Questions about a score: ask in class or on the LMS.
